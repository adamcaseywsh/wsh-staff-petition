Western State Hospital — Staff Petition

Voluntary staff participation guide

This is an independent petition initiative, not an official WSH or DBHDS communication.

  1. Read the staff petition and its proposed oversight measures in full.
  2. If you choose to support it, open the staff empowerment section of the website and select appropriate recipients.
  3. Copy the suggested email declaration. Edit it so it reflects only your own views and knowledge. You may also write your own message.
  4. Copy the subject and addresses into your email, check recipients, and send only if you wish.
  5. For sensitive allegations or evidence, use official confidential complaint/intake channels separately; do not include patient medical information in a group email.
  6. Optional paper signatures: download the petition PDF. Never post signed pages publicly or pressure coworkers to sign.
Privacy: Email is not anonymous. The website does not collect names or signatures, but network and hosting services may keep logs. Participation is voluntary; do not claim that other staff endorse the petition without their consent.
